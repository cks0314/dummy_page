AR-DPCBF guarantees collision-free navigation under bounded adversarial obstacle maneuvers while maintaining real-time computational performance.

Navigation Environment

Figure 1. Navigation environment considered in this work. (a) Bicycle-model robot with state and control inputs. (b) Relative robot-obstacle geometry used throughout the paper.

We consider a bicycle-model robot navigating toward a goal in an environment populated with independently maneuvering unicycle obstacles. Unlike constant-velocity obstacles, these agents may continuously change their speed and heading, producing time-varying relative motion with the robot. The robot must generate collision-free trajectories while respecting its nonholonomic dynamics and continuously reasoning about the relative position and velocity of nearby obstacles in real time.
