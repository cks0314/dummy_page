Controller Variants

Comparison of the four controller formulations proposed and evaluated in this work.

Method Constraint Objective Feasibility Guarantee
DPCBF
 \(\color{#64748b}{h_j}\) \(\color{#334155}{J_{\text{tr}}}\) Full CBF
Hard AR-DPCBF
 \(\color{#2563eb}{h_j^*}\) \(\color{#334155}{J_{\text{tr}}}\) Reduced A-CBF
Soft AR-DPCBF
 \(\color{#64748b}{h_j}\) \(\color{#334155}{J_{\text{tr}}} + \color{#059669}{J_{\text{soft}}}\) Full Best-effort
Buffer Soft AR-DPCBF
 \(\color{#64748b}{h_j}\) \(\color{#334155}{J_{\text{tr}}} + \color{#7c3aed}{J_{\text{buf}}}\) Full Best-effort

Definitions

\( \color{#64748b}{h_j} = \tilde v_{\text{rel},x} + \lambda\,\tilde v_{\text{rel},y}^2 + \mu \) \( \color{#2563eb}{h_j^*} = \tilde v_{\text{rel},x} + \lambda^*\,\tilde v_{\text{rel},y}^2 + \mu^* \)
\( \color{#64748b}{\lambda = k_\lambda \tfrac{d}{\|\boldsymbol{v}_{\text{rel}}\|}}, \ \ \color{#64748b}{\mu = k_\mu d} \) \( \color{#2563eb}{\lambda^* = \lambda-\Delta_\lambda}, \ \ \color{#2563eb}{\mu^* = \mu-\Delta_\mu} \) \( \Delta_\lambda = \tfrac{\kappa}{\gamma a_{\max}\|\boldsymbol{v}_{\text{rel}}\|}, \ \ \Delta_\mu = \tfrac{\kappa d}{\gamma\|\boldsymbol{v}_{\text{rel}}\|} \)
\( \color{#334155}{J_{\text{tr}} = \|\boldsymbol{u}-\boldsymbol{u}_{\text{ref}}\|^2} \) \( \color{#059669}{J_{\text{soft}} = \rho\textstyle\sum_j \max(0,-g_j)^2} \) \( \color{#7c3aed}{J_{\text{buf}} = \rho\textstyle\sum_j \phi(g_j;\varepsilon_b)} \)
Note. Hard AR-DPCBF enforces \(\color{#2563eb}{h_j^*} \geq 0\) as a hard constraint. Since \(\lambda^* \le \lambda\) and \(\mu^* \le \mu\), the adversarial safe set is a strict subset of the nominal one; the soft variants retain the nominal feasible set \(\{\color{#64748b}{h_j} \geq 0\}\) and move the adversarial condition \(g_j \geq 0\) into the objective as a penalty.
