The Fundamental Gap

DPCBF assumes the obstacle never maneuvers

Its safety-critical Lie derivative $L_f h$ evaluates $\dot x_{obs}$ as $v_{obs}[\cos\theta_{obs}, \sin\theta_{obs}]^\top$ — the current velocity, extended forever. When the obstacle really has acceleration $a_{obs}$ and turning rate $\omega_{obs}$, the true rate differs by $$ \Delta(t) = a_{obs}\cos\tilde\theta_{obs} \;+\; v_{obs}\,\omega_{obs} \Big[\,2\lambda\,\tilde v_{rel,y}\cos\tilde\theta_{obs} \;-\; \sin\tilde\theta_{obs}\,\Big], \qquad \tilde\theta_{obs} = \theta_{obs} - \alpha . $$ Whenever $\Delta(t) < 0$, the QP's constraint $L_f h + L_g h\,u \ge -\alpha(h)$ can hold on paper while the true barrier is already violated — the safety guarantee fails silently.
